Welcome to our journey

I’m writing this because I love you more than I know how to say out loud. I know I don’t always say the right things at the right times, and I worry that I don't show you just how much I love you every single day. Sometimes I look at you and I’m so overwhelmed by how lucky I am that I just don't have the words to portray it properly.

I wanted to give you something small that you can return to whenever the world feels a little too much. On the days you feel unappreciated or find yourself overthinking, I hope you find some peace in here. These aren't just words on paper; they are a million little reminders that you are loved, you are seen, and you are everything to me. You’ll never have to wonder how I feel again—it’s all right here.
