Travel the World

The Adventures Await I’m so ready to pack our bags and see the world with you. I want to experience different cultures, try new foods, and get lost in cities we’ve never heard of, knowing that as long as I’m holding your hand, I’m never truly lost. Whether it’s a spontaneous road trip or a long flight across the ocean, I want to see every sunset and every landmark through your eyes. I want our camera roll to be full of memories from places we once only dreamed of visiting.

🏡💍

Building Our Home

Building Our Home I don’t just want to "be" with you; I want to build a life that is entirely ours. I’m dreaming of the day we get to say "I do" and start our journey as husband and wife. I can see us picking out furniture for our first home, arguing over where the couch goes, and finally having a space that feels like our own sanctuary. I want to create a place where we feel safe, where we can be our truest selves, and where we start the family we’ve talked about.

🫂 ❤️

My sanctuary

I’m excited for the "forever" of it all, but I’m also ready for the reality of it. I want to be the person who holds you up when life gets heavy and the first person you run to when you have something to celebrate. I promise to support your dreams as if they were my own and to be the peace you come home to after a long day. No matter what challenges the world throws at us, I want us to be the ultimate team—always choosing each other, every single day.

🌅

Growing Old Together

Most of all, I’m excited to grow old with you. I want to see how we change over the decades and still be the one who knows exactly how to make you laugh. I imagine us years from now, still "awkwardly" cuddling on the couch, watching whatever the future version of Daz Games is, with a much older Bandit (or his successors) tucked between us. I want to look back on a lifetime of inside jokes and realize that the feeling I had the first night my heart skipped a beat for you never actually went away.
