I’m Sorry, and I Promise...

I’m writing this here because I want my apology to be as permanent as the love I have for you. I know I let you down, and I know that looking at those pages was a violation of the trust and the boundaries we built together. I was so caught up in the jokes and the funny comments with my brother that I completely lost sight of what I was actually looking at. I didn’t 'clock' it in the moment, but that’s no excuse. I should have been more mindful of how my actions would affect you, regardless of why I was on those pages.

It wasn't fair to you, and it doesn't even begin to reflect how I truly feel when I’m with you. I’ve realized that by mindlessly scrolling for a laugh, I was trading real, precious moments of our connection for empty pixels and cheap entertainment. You are the only person I want to build a future with, and the only person who truly has my heart.

I hate that my thoughtlessness made you feel like you had to compete with a screen or that you were anything less than perfect in my eyes. No comment section or joke is worth making you feel second-best. The truth is, no photo could ever capture the way you make me feel or the beauty you bring into my life every single day. You aren't just 'enough'—you are my world, my peace, and my greatest luck. I am so sorry for making you doubt, even for a second, that you are the only one I see.

I Pinky Promise:

  • Mindfullness: I promise to be more intentional with what I engage with online. I’ve realized that even if my intent is just 'looking at comments,' the content still matters. I commit to closing the app or hitting 'not interested' the moment something disrespectful appears, rather than lingering for a laugh.
  • Priority: I promise that my phone will never come before our peace. When we are together, I want to be 100% present with the woman who actually makes my life better, not distracted by people who don't even know I exist. I want to spend my energy making you laugh, not looking for jokes elsewhere.
  • Transparency: I promise to be an open book. I never want you to feel like you have to be a detective in your own relationship. I’m committing to total transparency—not because you're 'policing' me, but because I want you to feel so secure that you never feel the need to wonder what I'm doing.
  • AlgorithmI have already started resetting my digital world. I am actively teaching my social media that I only want to see things that build us up—travel ideas, hobbies we share, and things that remind me of our future. I’m purging the things that don't belong in our life.

Thank you for being so patient with me, even when I don’t deserve it. This situation has been a massive wake-up call for me; I’ve realized that I haven't been the best version of myself for you lately. I allowed myself to be mindless and thoughtless, and in doing so, I let down the person who matters most. I want to be a man who protects your heart, not someone who makes you feel like you have to guard it against me. I want to be a man you can be proud of—someone whose actions match his words every single day. You have shown me what real love and grace look like, and I am committed to growing into the man you truly deserve. I love you more than I have the words to say, and I hope that from here on out, my actions speak louder than any text on a screen ever could. You are my only priority, and you have my whole heart—today, tomorrow, and always

With all my love, forever.
