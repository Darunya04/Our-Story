The couch, Dog and the Flutter in my Heart

I want to tell you a story about a night you might remember, but from my perspective. It was back when everything was still new, and I was still trying to figure out how to be around you without giving away just how much I was already starting to care. We had just finished a long shift at work and headed back to my house. It was late, completely dark outside, and we were just relaxing on the couch with Daz Games playing on the TV. You had Bandit curled up right on your lap, and I remember looking at the two of you and thinking how perfectly you fit there. I’ll be honest—I totally used Bandit as my excuse to get closer to you. I leaned in, pretending I was just trying to pet him or be near him, but really, I was just trying to find a way to bridge the gap between us. I was so convinced I was being clumsy and that you’d notice how nervous I was. My heart was already thumping in my chest, and I was overthinking every single move. But then, the world just... stopped. As I finally settled in next to you, I felt your hand find mine. You started slowly caressing my hand with your thumb—just a tiny, rhythmic motion while we watched the screen. It was so soft and so intentional. In that exact second, I felt my heart skip a complete beat. It wasn’t just a physical spark; it was this sudden, overwhelming realization that you wanted to be there just as much as I did. All the noise in my head went quiet. Daz was laughing on the TV, Bandit was asleep, and the room was dark, but all I could feel was your thumb against my skin. In that moment, I realized I wasn’t just 'into' you. I was falling for you. I think a part of me has belonged to you since that very second on that couch, and I’ve never looked back.

The First Time

They say absence makes the heart grow fonder, but I didn't realize how true that was until my 21st birthday. Up until then, we had been spending every possible second together, but then I flew to Turkey for a week. Being a world away from you was the loudest silence I’ve ever experienced. Every beautiful view made me wish you were standing next to me, and every night I realized there was a huge, empty space where you were supposed to be. That week taught me that I didn't just like you—I needed you. When I finally got back, we went out to play pool before heading back to my house. We were in one of our moods, jokingly fighting. You looked at me and told me you 'hated' me. We both knew what that really meant—it was your way of hiding how much you’d missed me. I looked at you, and the 'I love you' was right there on the tip of my tongue, but I wasn't ready to play it safe. I told you that if that was the case, then I must 'despise' you. It was our own secret language. I knew that your 'hate' was actually love, and I wanted you to know that my 'despise' was the deepest love I had ever felt for anyone. It was the moment we finally said it without having to say it at all, and I’ve 'despised' you more and more every single day since.
